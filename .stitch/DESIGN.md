# Gas Lagba Admin Design System — Orange & White

## 1. Brand Identity & Colors
- **Primary Brand Color:** `#FF6600` (Vibrant Safety Flame Orange)
- **Primary Dark / Hover:** `#EA580C` (Deep Orange 600)
- **Primary Light / Background Tint:** `#FFF7ED` (Warm Orange 50 / Selection)
- **Primary Border / Outline:** `#FFEDD5` (Orange 100 / Active Focus)
- **Pure White Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crisp White for Cards & Panels)
- **Background Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` (Light Slate 50 Neutral Surface)
- **Neutral Dark (Text Primary):** `#0F172A` (Slate 900 / High Contrast Legibility)
- **Neutral Medium (Text Secondary):** `#475569` (Slate 600)
- **Neutral Muted:** `#94A3B8` (Slate 400)
- **Neutral Border:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200 / Subtle Container Edges)
- **Sidebar Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Crisp White Sidebar with `#FF6600` accents) or Deep Charcoal `#0F172A`
- **Success:** `#10B981` (Emerald 500)
- **Warning:** `#F59E0B` (Amber 500)
- **Danger:** `#EF4444` (Red 500)

## 2. Typography
- **Font Family:** `Geist, Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
- **Headings:** Bold, letter-spacing `-0.02em`, `#0F172A`
- **Body Text:** Regular 14px / 1.5 line-height
- **Currency & Tabular Numbers:** Bold Monospace / Tabular figures (`৳1,250.00`)

## 3. UI Component Architecture
- **Primary Buttons:** High-intent `#FF6600` solid background with crisp white text and smooth `#EA580C` hover.
- **Secondary Buttons:** Pure white background with 1px border `border-slate-200` and `#0F172A` text, hover `#FFF7ED` with orange border.
- **Cards & Data Tables:** Pure white containers (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-xl`, subtle 1px border `border-slate-200`, slight `shadow-xs`.
- **Active Navigation Items:** `#FFF7ED` light orange pill with vibrant `#FF6600` text and vertical indicator bar.
